Big Five self-reflection

Athlete Personality Profile

This helps you and your coach understand how you naturally think, feel and work with others, so coaching and feedback can fit you.

  • There are no right or wrong answers and no good or bad personality.
  • This is not a test, and it is not used for team selection.
  • Answer as you usually are now, not how you wish to be, and not only in sport.
  • Go with your first reaction. 50 short statements, about 10 minutes.

How to answer

For each statement, pick how well it describes you.

1Very inaccurate
2Moderately inaccurate
3Neither
4Moderately accurate
5Very accurate

Example: if "I start conversations" fits you fairly well, pick 4.

Keep in mind

  • This is a snapshot of how you see yourself today. Treat it as ideas to explore, not fixed facts.
  • The score bands are a practical coaching guide, not official population norms.
  • This is a self-awareness tool, not a clinical test. If you are going through a hard time, talk to your coach or a qualified sport psychologist or mental health professional.
  • Doing it again in 6 to 12 months is a good way to reflect on change.
